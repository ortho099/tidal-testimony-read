Series Premise

On a torrential night in Sai Wan, independent forensic investigator Shen Zhiwei comes face to face with Lu Chenzhou, the Wall Street crisis partner flown in from New York to seal the files on a seven-year-old trust collapse. She is hunting for the truth and her vanished brother; his mandate is to lock the archive forever. Across a mahogany desk—and seven unspoken years—two formidable rivals circle each other through Hong Kong, Shanghai, and New York until the receding tide reveals the missing eighth page.
